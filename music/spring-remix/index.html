Spring (Yotah's Healing Remix)

I was captivated by the original track's 'airy' feel, particularly in the vocals and the overall mix. The rhythmic patterns were so intricate and refined that I didn't dare touch them. Instead, I focused on translating that spacey, airy vibe into a spherical drone. My goal was to create something reminiscent of lying on the grass on a warm day, watching clouds drift by, a healing sensation I tend to forget during long winters and rediscover each spring.
