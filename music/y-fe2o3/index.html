y-Fe2O3

y-Fe2O3 was composed explicitly for tape. The warm, slightly distorted sound, the soft hiss, the fluctuation of playback speed creates sonic characteristics that are missed today and are therefore deliberately simulated.

more

The piece explores experimental composition through intentional tape simulation, combining spherical sound and hard hitting beats with a soft carpet of white noise. The second track contains artifacts resulting from the medium itself rather than deliberate composition.

Composition and mixing by Yves Spiri Mastering by Benoît Piccand Artwork by Julia Häller Additional graphic design by Simon Lanz Released by CRTTR
