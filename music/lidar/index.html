LiDAR EP

After experiencing numerous collective moments on dance floors, Yotah and Voicheck were inspired to share their own take on bass music. Blending fast-paced rhythms with gritty basslines and a touch of experimental sound design, 'LiDAR' carries a heavy, yet playful energy, originating from their deep-rooted friendship.

Composition, Mixing, Mastering by Yotah & Voicheck Artwork by Voicheck Released by CRTTR
